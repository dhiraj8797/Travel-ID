Travel ID is your on-device travel wallet. Upload tickets and hotel bookings, then open boarding-style passes whenever you need them — even offline.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Add train, flight, bus, and hotel bookings from PDFs or photos
• Beautiful boarding passes with QR / barcode for check-in
• Live train status, coach view, and GPS speed on the go
• Hotel stays with room number you can add or edit after check-in
• Google Sign-In to keep your wallet tied to your account on this device
• Arrival reminders so you don’t miss your stop

PRIVACY FIRST
• Boarding passes are stored locally on your phone
• We don’t sync your wallet to our servers
• You control what you share
