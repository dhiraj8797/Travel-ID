Everything in the app

Every option on Home, Passes, Add, and Profile — listed here so you know what you’re getting.

Wallet & home

  • Home wallet — Recent passes, category filters (All / Bus / Train / Flight / Hotel / Metro).
  • Passes tab — Full list of saved boarding passes, open any time.
  • Add location — Optional home location chip for local context.
  • Yearly travel expense — Quick view of spend from tickets you’ve saved.

Live while you travel

  • Live train status — Running status, delay, and ETA without leaving the pass.
  • Coach map — See where your coach sits on the train.
  • GPS speed meter — On-the-go speed while you’re moving.
  • Live flight status — Gate-side updates for your flight.
  • Arrival alarm — Reminder ~20 minutes before your station so you don’t miss the stop.
  • Night headlamps — Bus and train scenes light up after sunset (IST).

Metro (India)

  • City by GPS — Auto-detect Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Pune, Ahmedabad, Kochi, Lucknow, Jaipur, Nagpur, and more.
  • Station pickers — Choose From and To on your city’s network.
  • Official gate QR — Keep WhatsApp / Namma-style metro QR ready for entry.

Account & privacy

  • Google or email sign-in — Keep your wallet tied to your account on this device.
  • Encrypted vault — Passes stored on-device with AES-256-GCM; not synced to our servers.
  • Screenshot block — Boarding barcodes and metro gate QRs stay off screenshots.
  • Profile & settings — Reminders, cache clear, delete passes, sign out, legal docs.
